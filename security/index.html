How we protect your data

Updated October 27, 2026

Cohabit handles card transactions, so security is part of how the app is built. Here is what we do, in plain terms.

We see transactions only

Cohabit reads the date, amount, merchant and category of charges on the cards you connect, plus the account name and last four digits. We never see your bank username or password, and we do not collect account numbers, balances, identity documents or income. Cohabit cannot move money.

Bank connections go through Plaid

You connect a bank on Plaid's own screens. Plaid gives Cohabit an access key for the connection; that key is encrypted again before it is stored, stays on our server, and is never sent to your phone. Unlinking a bank in the app removes the connection at Plaid.

Everything is encrypted

Every connection uses TLS 1.2 or newer. Our database and backups are encrypted at rest (AES-256), and bank access keys are encrypted a second time. On your phone, saved data is protected by iOS and left out of device backups.

Only your household can see your data

Access rules are enforced by the database itself: a person can only read their own household, and only the charges they are allowed to see. People outside your household see nothing of yours unless you share a specific tab with them.

Signing in

You sign in with Apple, which requires two-factor authentication, or with a one-time code sent to your email. There are no passwords to leak. A Face ID, Touch ID or passcode lock, on by default, protects the app on your phone.

How we run things

  • Cohabit runs on managed cloud infrastructure in the United States; we operate no servers of our own.
  • Administrative access requires multi-factor authentication and is reviewed regularly.
  • Every code change is tested automatically before release, and dependencies are monitored for known vulnerabilities.
  • We review our security policy and risks every six months, and after any incident.

Your data, your choice

You can export your charges, unlink a bank or delete your account from inside the app. We never sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

To report a security concern, email cohabitdevdata@gmail.com with "Security" in the subject line.
