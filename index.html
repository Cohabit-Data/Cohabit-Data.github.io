Money you share, kept simple.

Cohabit is an iPhone app for couples and housemates. Your card charges arrive on their own, you sort each one as shared or yours, and you always know who owes whom.

Charges come to youConnect the cards you already use. New charges show up by themselves.
Sort in a tapShared, yours or theirs. Cohabit learns your habits and suggests the answer.
Settle up easilyOne running balance, split the way you choose, with Venmo, Cash App or PayPal a tap away.
Read-only, alwaysCohabit sees transactions only. It never has your bank login and cannot move money.

Your data

Bank connections go through Plaid, so your bank sign-in never reaches Cohabit. Your data is encrypted, only your household can see it, and you can delete your account from inside the app at any time.
