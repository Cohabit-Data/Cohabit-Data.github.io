Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026 · Questions: cohabitdevdata@gmail.com

Cohabit helps you and the people you live with see what you spend and who owes whom. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, and either your Sign in with Apple identifier or your email address.
  • Your household: who is in it, how you split costs, your budgets, goals, lists, tags and shared tabs, and the payment handles you choose to share (a Venmo, Cash App or PayPal name).
  • Your photo, if you add one: shown to the people in your household in place of your initial. You can remove it at any time in Settings.
  • Your Pip (the companion character in the app): its colour, outfit, room, level and coins, the days you opened Cohabit, and your Pip code, so it follows you to a new phone and friends you add can visit it.
  • Notifications, if you allow them: a token from Apple that lets our server send notifications to your phone. A notification names the person involved and, for a question about a charge, that charge's merchant and amount.
  • Crash and hang reports, only if you share them: Apple's diagnostic reports stay on your phone unless you choose to send them to us. They contain no names or financial details.
  • Bank data you choose to connect, through Plaid: the names and last four digits of your accounts; transactions (date, amount, merchant, category, and the merchant details Plaid provides, such as location, website, logo and category code, the currency, and a payment's method and reference); and payments made to your credit cards, taken from the cards' own transactions. We do not collect bank passwords, account or routing numbers, the account holder's name, income or identity data.
  • How you use Cohabit: which screens you open and a few actions you take (for example, that you connected a bank or settled up, or that an error appeared), with the app and iOS version. Never amounts, merchants, names, notes or anything you typed. This is kept by Cohabit itself, with no third-party analytics service, for 90 days and then only as daily totals. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & lock → Help improve Cohabit.

How bank connections work

You connect a bank through Plaid, which presents its own screens and consent. Your bank sign-in goes to Plaid or to your bank, never to Cohabit. Plaid's handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. Plaid gives Cohabit an access key for each connection, which we store encrypted on our server and never on your phone.

How we use your data

We use your data only to show you and your household your own spending, budgets and balances; to suggest categories (worked out on your phone); to keep your phones in sync; and, from the usage information described above, to understand what is working and what is not so that we can improve the app. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • People in your household see shared charges and the charges you mark as theirs. They see your cards and personal charges only if you turn on Share my cards and personal charges, and never a charge you mark Keep private.
  • Friends on a shared tab you send a link to can see that tab: its expenses, payments and who owes whom, and, when they owe you, the payment handle you provided. Nothing else of yours.
  • Pip friends (people you have exchanged Pip codes with, and the people you live with) see your first name and your Pip: its look, room and level, the days in the last five weeks you opened Cohabit, and the greetings you send each other. Never your money, your coins or anything from your household. Either of you can remove the other at any time.
  • Other households see nothing of yours.
  • Our service providers store data on our behalf: Supabase (database and sign-in, United States), Plaid (bank connections) and Apple (Sign in with Apple, notifications and TestFlight).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest; bank access keys are encrypted separately; access rules are enforced by the database; and an optional Face ID, Touch ID or passcode lock protects the app on your phone. Our security page describes these practices in more detail.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as you use Cohabit. Anything you delete stays in Recently deleted for 30 days so it can be restored, and is then permanently removed from our server. Settings → Account → Export charges gives you a spreadsheet of every charge you can see. You can unlink a bank at any time under Settings → Banks & cards.

Settings → Account → Delete my account disconnects your banks at Plaid, deletes your cards, your sign-in and the charges that were yours alone, and takes effect immediately. Charges you shared remain in your household's history, attributed to a former member, because they are part of what others owe or are owed. If you are the last person in the household, everything is deleted.

Children

Cohabit is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell you in the app about any significant change.
