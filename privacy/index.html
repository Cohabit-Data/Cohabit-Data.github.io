Cohabit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · Contact: cohabitdevdata@gmail.com

Cohabit helps you and the people you live with see what you spend and who owes whom. This page says what we collect, why, who sees it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, and either your Sign in with Apple identifier or your email address.
  • Your household: who is in it, your split, budgets, goals, lists, tags and shared tabs you create, and the ways you said you can be paid (a Venmo, Cash App or PayPal name).
  • Your photo, if you add one: shown to the people in your household in place of your initial. Remove it any time in Settings.
  • Your Pip: its colour, what it wears, its room, its level and coins, the days you opened Cohabit, and your Pip code — kept so it follows you to a new phone and so friends you add can visit it.
  • Notifications (if you allow them): a token from Apple that lets our server send this phone a notification. Notifications say a name, and for a question about a charge, its merchant and amount.
  • Crash and hang reports (only if you share them): Apple's diagnostic reports stay on your phone unless you tap Share diagnostics. They contain no names or money.
  • Bank data you choose to connect, through Plaid: your accounts' names and last four digits; transactions (date, amount, merchant, category, and what Plaid adds about the merchant: its location, website, logo and category code, the currency, and a payment's method and reference); payments made to your credit cards (from the cards' own transactions). We do not collect bank passwords, account or routing numbers, the account holder's name, or income or identity data.
  • How you use Cohabit: which pages you open and a few things you do (for example, that you linked a bank or settled up, or that something went wrong on screen), with the app and iOS version. Never amounts, merchants, names, notes or anything you typed. Kept by Cohabit itself — no analytics company — for 90 days, then only as daily totals. Turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy → Help improve Cohabit.

How bank connections work

You connect a bank through Plaid, which shows its own screens and consent. Your bank sign-in goes to Plaid (or your bank), never to Cohabit. Plaid's handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. Plaid gives Cohabit an access key, which we store encrypted on our server and never on your phone.

How we use it

Only to show you and your household your own spending, budgets and balances, to suggest categories (worked out on your phone), to keep your phones in sync, and — from how Cohabit is used — to see what's working and what isn't so we can improve it. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • People in your household see shared charges and what you've marked as theirs. They see your cards and personal charges only if you turn on Share my cards and charges, and never a charge you mark Keep private.
  • Friends on a shared tab you send a link to see that tab — its expenses, payments and who owes whom — and, when they owe you, the Venmo, Cash App or PayPal name you gave. Nothing else of yours.
  • Pip friends — people you've swapped Pip codes with, and the people you live with — see your first name and your Pip: its look, its room, its level, the days in the last five weeks you opened Cohabit, and the hellos (a wave, a hug, a high five, good vibes) you send each other. Never your money, coins or anything from your household. Either of you can remove the other.
  • People in other households see nothing else of yours.
  • Our service providers store data for us: Supabase (database and sign-in, United States), Plaid (bank connections), Apple (Sign in with Apple, notifications, TestFlight).

Security

Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest; bank access keys encrypted separately; access rules enforced by the database; an optional Face ID lock. See our security practices.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you use Cohabit. Something you delete stays in Recently deleted for 30 days so it can be put back, then it is removed from our server too. Settings → Account → Export charges gives you a spreadsheet of everything you can see. You can unlink a bank at any time (Settings → Banks). Settings → Account → Delete my account disconnects your banks at Plaid, deletes your cards and charges that were yours alone, and deletes your sign-in. Shared charges stay in your household's history (under "Former member") because they are part of what others owe or are owed; if you are the last person in the household, everything is deleted.

Children

Cohabit is not for people under 18.

Changes

We'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
