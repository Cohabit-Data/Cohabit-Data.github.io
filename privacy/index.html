Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026 · cohabitdevdata@gmail.com

Cohabit helps you and the people you live with see what you spend and who owes whom. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, and either your Sign in with Apple identifier or your email address.
  • Your household: who is in it, how you split costs, your budgets, goals, lists, tags and shared tabs, and any payment handle you choose to share (a Venmo, Cash App or PayPal name).
  • Your profile photo, if you add one, and your Pip (the companion character in the app): its look, level and the days you opened Cohabit.
  • Bank data you choose to connect, through Plaid: the names and last four digits of your accounts, and your transactions (date, amount, merchant, category and the merchant details Plaid provides). We do not collect bank passwords, account or routing numbers, income or identity data.
  • Notifications, if you allow them: a token from Apple so our server can notify your phone.
  • How you use Cohabit: which screens you open and a few actions you take, with the app and iOS version. Never amounts, merchants, names or anything you typed. Kept by Cohabit itself for 90 days, then only as daily totals. Turn it off in Settings → Privacy & lock.

How bank connections work

You connect a bank through Plaid, on Plaid's own screens. Your bank sign-in goes to Plaid or your bank, never to Cohabit. Plaid's handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. Plaid gives Cohabit an access key for the connection, which we store encrypted on our server.

How we use your data

Only to show you and your household your own spending, budgets and balances, to suggest categories, to keep your phones in sync, and to understand which parts of the app are used so we can improve it. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Your household sees shared charges and the charges you mark as theirs. They see your cards and personal charges only if you turn on Share my cards and personal charges, and never a charge you mark Keep private.
  • Friends you share a tab with see that tab only: its expenses, payments, who owes whom, and the payment handle you provided.
  • Pip friends see your first name and your Pip, never your money.
  • Nobody else. Our service providers store data on our behalf: Supabase (database and sign-in, United States), Plaid (bank connections) and Apple (Sign in with Apple, notifications and TestFlight).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, bank access keys are encrypted separately, and access rules are enforced by the database. Our security page has more.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you use Cohabit. Anything you delete stays in Recently deleted for 30 days, then is permanently removed. You can export your charges (Settings → Account → Export charges) and unlink a bank (Settings → Banks & cards) at any time.

Settings → Account → Delete my account disconnects your banks at Plaid and deletes your cards, your sign-in and the charges that were yours alone, immediately. Charges you shared stay in your household's history, attributed to a former member, because they are part of what others owe or are owed. If you are the last person in the household, everything is deleted.

Children

Cohabit is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, we update the date above and tell you in the app about significant changes. Questions or requests about your data: cohabitdevdata@gmail.com.
